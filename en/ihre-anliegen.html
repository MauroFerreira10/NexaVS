Systemic strategy development
Change needs strategy - flexible, future-oriented and systemic.
Traditional strategy models that rely on predictability and linear planning are reaching their limits in today's dynamic world. Rapid technological developments, economic uncertainties, stricter sustainability requirements and changing customer needs require new, adaptable strategies or even a comprehensive reorganization. Companies must learn to navigate uncertainty without losing their direction.

We help you to develop robust and flexible strategies and structures that provide orientation even in turbulent times, ensure the ability to act and enable long-term value creation.

Fields of action

01
Developing an agile and resilient business model
Developing future-proof business models through scenario analyses, business model innovation and iterative strategy loops.
Establish agile organizational structures for greater adaptability to market changes.
Accompany digital transformation to use technology as a driver for efficiency & innovation.
02
Designing systemic change management for sustainable change
Take a holistic view of change processes - including people (culture & people) and processes (organizational structure & process organization).
Develop managers as change agents to anchor transformation in the company.
Use participative methods such as Design Thinking, Dynamic Facilitation & systemic workshops for broad acceptance.
03
Implementing reorganization effectively
Adapt structures and roles to new strategic requirements in a targeted manner - without paralyzing the operational business.
Participation-oriented re-design processes that promote clarity, responsibility and motivation.
Accompany reorganization as cultural change - not just moving boxes, but enabling real effectiveness.
04
Promoting a culture of innovation & digital leadership
Establish a mindset shift for a corporate culture that promotes innovation.
Use of AI & automation to increase efficiency and enable new business models.
Data-Driven Decision Making: using data-based strategy development & smart analysis tools.
05
Strengthen customer focus
Optimize customer experience design to create personalized experiences.
Develop omnichannel strategies that intelligently link digital & physical touchpoints.
Enable co-creation with customers to make products and services more market-oriented.
06
Countering the shortage of skilled workers with systemic HR strategies
Strengthen employer branding to retain top talent in the long term.
Implement New Work approaches (agile methods, remote work, flexible structures, sense of purpose).
Establish employee participation & ownership models to increase motivation & innovative strength.
07
Integrating sustainability & ESG into the strategy
Introduce circular economy models to use resources more efficiently.
Embed ESG criteria in business decisions (impact assessment, CO₂ reduction/neutrality, social responsibility).
Develop sustainable supply chain strategies to minimize risks and create competitive advantages.
08
Ensuring systemic crisis prevention & risk management
Early warning systems & scenario planning to proactively manage crises.
Develop resilience strategies to make supply chains and business models crisis-proof.
Strengthen corporate governance & compliance to minimize legal and ethical risks.

Cultural development
Corporate culture as the foundation for (financial) success, employer attractiveness, innovation and willingness to change.
A strong corporate culture is not a product of chance - it is created through conscious design - and always begins with leadership. However, many companies struggle with resistance, silo thinking or a lack of responsibility. Managers are faced with the challenge of establishing a value-based, motivating and sustainable culture.

With our systemic approach, our view of processes and structures as well as mindset and attitude, we help you to develop a corporate culture that strengthens innovation, collaboration and personal responsibility - and makes your company attractive to employees and customers alike.

Guidance
Leadership and management structures that inspire & activate.
Leadership has changed - and with it the expectations of managers. It is no longer about rigid hierarchies or control, but about meaning, orientation and effectiveness. Leadership in the 21st century means consciously exemplifying values, empowering people and shaping collaboration on an equal footing.

New Work, agility and self-organization are not just methods, but an expression of a fundamental cultural change. If you want to be successful as a manager, you need a modern understanding of leadership that combines empathy, a sense of purpose, clear framework conditions and knowledge of modern management methods.

Fields of action

01
Value-oriented leadership and purpose-driven leadership
Development of a management culture based on clarity, meaningfulness and authenticity.
Establishing shared values and a clear vision as a guideline for decisions.
Promoting leadership that inspires, motivates and delegates responsibility to employees.
02
New Work, agility, collegial leadership, self-organization
Designing flexible, low-hierarchy structures that promote personal responsibility and innovation.
Implementation of agile methods and decision-making formats in order to act quickly and adaptively.
Supporting teams in the development of self-organization and new ways of working.
03
Modern feedback and evaluation systems
Mindset shift at management level for a corporate culture that promotes innovation.
Sharpening the focus on both results orientation and cultural development.
Establishing new discussion formats that focus on personal and professional development as well as individual contributions to the success of the team.
04
Empathic and effective leadership culture
Development of leaders with high social and emotional intelligence.
Strengthening psychological security to promote confidence and the courage to change.
Training on empathic communication, conflict resolution and appreciative cooperation.
05
Working on attitude and mindset
Reflection on your own beliefs that guide you as a leader.
Development of a growth mindset: focus on learning (including from mistakes) and professional and personal development.
Create the conditions for cooperation based on resonance and support rather than competition and rivalry.
06
Formats: Individual and group coaching, method training, peer coaching
Individual 1:1 coaching for managers to master personal challenges.
Group coaching and peer formats to learn from and with each other and to further develop leadership in practice.
Method training on modern management approaches that can be integrated directly into everyday life.

Results

Achieving goals through modern leadership

Clear, value-oriented leadership increases productivity, improves decision-making processes and creates a culture of personal responsibility.

Greater attractiveness as an employer

Modern, agile management approaches promote self-organization and motivation - and make your company more attractive to talented specialists.

More innovation and sustainable success

Empathetic and effective leadership creates an environment in which teams work creatively, develop further and make a sustainable contribution to the company's success.

Sustainability
Sustainability as corporate responsibility & strategic competitive advantage.
Sustainability is no longer a "nice-to-have", but a decisive competitive factor. Regulatory requirements are increasing, customers and employees are demanding responsibility, and companies are facing the challenge of effectively integrating sustainability into their strategy. However, there is often a lack of clear orientation: Where to start? Which measures have a real impact? And how can sustainability be implemented economically without jeopardizing profitability?

We help organizations to see sustainability not just as an obligation, but as a strategic opportunity. Our approach combines corporate value creation with ecological and social responsibility - in a practical, economical and measurable way.

Fields of action

01
Sustainability strategy & ESG integration
Development of a sustainable corporate strategy that clearly anchors ESG criteria (environmental, social, governance).
Derivation of specific targets and measures to reduce environmental and social risks and to fulfill governance requirements.
Combining sustainability with economic success through future-proof business models.
02
Making sustainability measurable
Making sustainability measurable Introduction of KPIs and monitoring systems to measure sustainable progress.
Development of a pragmatic approach for sustainability controlling and impact assessment.
Use of data-based tools to identify optimization potential.
03
Regulatory requirements & reporting obligations
Support with the implementation of legal requirements (omnibus, CSRD, CSDDD, EU taxonomy, supply chain law, etc.).
Development of efficient processes to fulfill transparency and reporting obligations.
Support in the preparation of sustainability reports with meaningful KPIs for stakeholders and investors.
04
Innovation & sustainable business models
Use of sustainability as a differentiating feature for customers and markets.
Identification of new market opportunities through sustainable product and service innovations.
Development of sustainable business models that integrate environmental and social responsibility.
05
Sustainable corporate culture & leadership
Raising awareness and empowering managers to embed sustainability in the company.
Development of employee engagement programs and change management processes.
Promoting a sustainable corporate culture through values, communication and behavior.
06
Sustainable supply chains & impact sourcing
Development of transparent, sustainable supply chains with responsible partners.
Introduction of sustainability standards and certifications for suppliers.
Minimizing social and ecological risks in global value chains.
07
Circular economy & resource conservation
Development of concepts for a sustainable value chain that thinks in cycles and not linearly.
Promotion of the 4-R principles: Reduce, Reuse, Replace & Recycle with all stakeholders in the value chain.
Strategies for resource efficiency, CO₂ reduction and sustainable energy use across the entire value chain.

Results

Competitiveness & future security

Sustainability strengthens the market position, increases attractiveness for customers and investors and ensures long-term resilience to regulatory and economic changes.

Efficiency & cost reduction

Sustainable processes, a circular economy and energy efficiency help to conserve resources and reduce operating costs, which leads to higher profitability. Sustainability and profitability are not contradictory - they complement each other and go hand in hand.

Attractiveness as an employer & employee motivation

Companies with clear sustainability strategies and goals are attractive to committed talent, increase employee identification and promote a positive corporate culture.
